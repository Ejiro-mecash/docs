---
title: 'Fetch Batch Status'
description: 'Retrieve the processing status and results of a bulk payout batch.'
openapi: get /v1/bulk/payout/{batchId}/status
---

Use the `batchId` returned by the [Create Bulk Payout](/payout/create-bulk-payout) endpoint to retrieve the current processing status of the batch and the status of each individual payout within it.
